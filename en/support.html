Frequently asked questions

How do credits work?

Monthly Premium provides 15 credits and Yearly Premium provides 60 credits after each successful purchase or renewal. Effects and poses cost 3 credits; basic enhancement tools cost 1 credit. The cost appears before an operation starts.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

On iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Fixel. Cancellation does not end access before the current period expires. Fixel cannot directly cancel subscriptions purchased through Apple.

My purchase is missing. What should I do?

Confirm that you use the same Fixel account and Apple Account. Select Restore Purchases on the paywall or Settings screen. Restoring verifies an existing entitlement; it does not add new credits.

A job failed but credits were deducted.

Refresh Projects and your balance. If a credit for a job that produced no result was not returned automatically, contact us with your account email, the approximate time and the feature used. You do not need to send the photo itself.

Why did my photo not look as expected?

Use a recent, unfiltered, well-lit selfie with a clearly visible face. Face-covering accessories, very low resolution or multiple people can affect the result.

How do I delete my account and data?

Start a deletion request from in-app account controls or contact us from your registered email. Cancel any active App Store subscription separately in Apple Account settings.

How do I request a refund?

Apple processes payments and decides refund requests. Submit a request at reportaproblem.apple.com.
