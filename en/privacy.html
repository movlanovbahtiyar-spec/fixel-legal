Privacy Policy

This policy explains how personal data is collected, used, shared and protected when you use the Fixel mobile app.

Effective date: September 29, 2026

1. Controller and scope

Fixel (“Fixel,” “we,” “us”) is an AI-powered photo editing app provided by Bahtiyar Movlanov. This policy applies to Fixel accounts, the mobile app, subscriptions, credits and support services.

For privacy requests, contact movlanovbahtiyar@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

Account and profile information

Name, email address, the identifier or private relay email provided by Sign in with Apple, app user ID, optional profile name and profile photo.

Photos and user content

Selfies and photos you upload or capture; selected poses, effects and editing preferences; object-removal descriptions or drawing masks; generated results; and content you share in a support request.

Subscription, transaction and credit information

Apple product identifiers, transaction and subscription status, purchase and renewal history, entitlement status, credit balance and credit activity. Fixel does not receive your payment card details; Apple processes payments.

Technical and usage information

Limited technical information required to operate the service, such as device and operating-system type, app version, security and error logs, processing time and features used. Our providers may process network data such as IP addresses in their own security logs.

3. Why we use data

  • To create, verify and manage your account;
  • To perform the photo editing, pose, effect, enhancement and object-removal actions you request;
  • To verify Premium subscriptions, maintain credit balances and prevent double spending;
  • To associate photos and projects with your account and enable access across devices;
  • For troubleshooting, abuse prevention, security and customer support;
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

Processing is based on performance of our contract, your request or consent, our legitimate interests in security and service quality, and applicable legal obligations.

4. Photos and facial content

Fixel is not a facial-recognition or identity-verification system. Photos are processed only to produce the visual operation you request. We do not create biometric templates to identify you, match you against other databases or infer sensitive traits.

Only upload photos containing you or other people when you have the necessary rights and permissions. When you start an AI feature, the relevant photo may be transmitted over a secure connection to the selected processing provider solely to fulfil that request.

5. Service providers and international transfers

We do not sell personal data or share it for third-party advertising. Only the data necessary to provide the service may be sent to:

  • Supabase: authentication, databases, file storage and server functions;
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, App Store payments, receipts and subscription management;
  • RevenueCat: subscription status, transaction events and entitlement management;
  • OpenAI, Google Gemini and fal.ai: visual or text-based AI processing, depending on the feature you choose.

Not every photo is sent to every provider; only the provider required for the selected feature is used. These providers may process data on servers outside Türkiye or your country under their own contractual and data-processing terms.

6. Retention and deletion

Account and profile data may be retained while your account remains active. Uploaded and generated content may remain until you delete it, delete your account, or it is no longer required to provide the service. Security logs are retained for a limited period. Transaction and credit-ledger records may be kept as required for fraud prevention, accounting and legal obligations.

Temporary copies held by AI providers are subject to their security and retention terms. You may request deletion of your account and associated content through in-app account controls or by email. Deleting an account does not automatically cancel an active App Store subscription; cancel it separately in your Apple Account settings.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including encryption in transit, access controls, authorisation rules and server-side credit validation. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data; restrict processing; request portability; withdraw consent; and complain to a competent data-protection authority. Submit requests from the email associated with your account; we may need to verify your identity.

9. Children’s privacy

Fixel is not directed to children under 13. If the digital-consent age in your location is higher, parental or guardian permission is required. Contact us if you believe a child’s data has been processed without appropriate permission.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the product or law changes. We will provide appropriate notice of material changes and update the effective date.

Questions and data-subject requests: movlanovbahtiyar@gmail.com
